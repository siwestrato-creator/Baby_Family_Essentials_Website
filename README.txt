BABY & FAMILY ESSENTIALS — WEBSITE PACKAGE

1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE.co.za in:
   - index.html
   - sitemap.xml
   - robots.txt

2. Enter your REAL prices in prices.js.
   Prices were intentionally NOT invented.

3. Upload the entire folder to your web host. A normal HTTPS website is recommended.

4. After the site is live:
   - Set up Google Search Console for the domain.
   - Submit sitemap.xml.
   - Use URL Inspection to request indexing.
   Google says indexing can take several days and is not guaranteed to happen immediately.

5. For Google local visibility, create/claim your Google Business Profile using the same
   business name, phone number and physical address shown on the website.

6. The website includes:
   - Mobile-friendly design
   - Product search and category filters
   - Product images
   - Price display
   - WhatsApp order buttons
   - Call button
   - Google Maps link/embed
   - LocalBusiness structured data
   - Product catalogue structure
   - SEO title/description
   - Sitemap and robots.txt

IMPORTANT:
- The working business name "Baby & Family Essentials South Africa" is a placeholder.
  Change it to your real trading name before publishing.
- Do not publish a price until it is the actual price you intend to charge.
- Safety-sensitive, electrical, food-contact and branded products should only be sold/listed
  after you have confirmed the applicable supplier specifications and documentation.
